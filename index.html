About

Emanuele Errichiello is a Fellow in the Department of International Relations at the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), where he teaches on the BA in International Relations and on an MSc course in European affairs. He also convenes and lectures “The New Security Agenda” at INSTEP London, Wake Forest University. He is completing his ESRC-funded PhD in the same department.

His research focuses on EU external relations, the security–migration nexus and Euro-Mediterranean relations. Drawing on International Political Sociology and a neo-Bourdieusian approach, his work examines the practices, professionals and struggles that shape European policy towards its southern neighbourhood.

Emanuele has also worked across research and policy. He was Deputy Director and EU Programme Coordinator at the Centro Studi Internazionali (CSI) and Editor-in-Chief of the journal Studi Internazionali, and he served as Legislative Assistant to the Italian Senate’s XIV Committee on EU Affairs. He has been a Research Assistant at the LSE and at Interpeace.

He holds an MSc in European Public Policy from the LSE and a BA from the University of Kent, and is an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA).
