About

Emanuele Errichiello is a Fellow in the Department of International Relations at the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), where he teaches on European and international security at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. He is completing his ESRC-funded PhD in the same department. He also convenes and lectures an undergraduate course in international security at Wake Forest University (London campus). In 2025, he became an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy.

His research broadly focuses on EU & European foreign and security policy, Euro-Mediterranean relations, the Mediterranean as a region, and North Africa. His work examines, on the one hand, the processes of making and unmaking of regions and international spaces; on the other, the practices and struggles that shape European policies towards its southern neighbourhood.

Emanuele also works across non-academic research and policy. He is Deputy Director at the Centro Studi Internazionali (CSI) – an Italian think tank – and has held various policy advising and advocacy roles in Italy.
