Research

How the European Union governs migration and security in its southern neighbourhood, and how those policies are contested.

Doctoral thesis

[Thesis title]

The thesis examines how the EU’s migration and security agenda is produced and contested in the Maghreb. It combines field theory with fieldwork in Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco.

Research themes

  • EU external relations

    How the EU builds and adjusts its policy towards the southern neighbourhood, from the Barcelona Process to the Pact for the Mediterranean.

  • The security–migration nexus

    Externalisation, border cooperation and the professionals who turn mobility into a security question.

  • Field theory in International Relations

    Using Bourdieusian concepts to study power, expertise and change in transnational policy fields.
